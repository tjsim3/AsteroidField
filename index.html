ASTEROID FIELD

The game of dodging asteroids!

How to play: Up / Down (or W / S) to move, Space (or Shift) to shoot. Grab dollar bills (+$50), bullets and power-ups! A glowing health pack flies by every 30 seconds - grab it to heal +1 (each pack arrives a second later than the last). Multiplayer: Player 1 = W/S + Shift · Player 2 = Arrows + Space.

Tip: open Drops in the Store to collect skins, then click a collected skin to equip it.
